Account Deletion

Shetty's Canteen mobile application

Last updated: [31 October 2026]

You can request deletion of your Shetty's Canteen account and associated personal data. This page explains how to submit a request, what data is deleted, and what information may need to be retained.

1. Delete your account in the App

  1. Open the Shetty's Canteen app and sign in to your customer account.
  2. Open the Profile section.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Review the information and consequences shown on screen, then confirm your request.
  5. Complete any identity or security verification requested by the App.

If you cannot access your account or cannot submit the request in the App, use the contact method in section 2.

2. Request deletion by email

Send an email to shettyscanteen.contact@gmail.com with the subject Shetty's Canteen – Account Deletion Request. Include the email address or mobile number registered with your account so we can locate it. Do not send your password or OTP.

We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request. We will not ask you to disclose your password.

3. What happens when your account is deleted

After we verify and process your request, we will delete or anonymise personal information associated with your account that is no longer needed. This may include:

  • your account profile details, such as name, gender, email address and mobile number;
  • your profile photograph and associated profile data;
  • device notification tokens associated with your account; and
  • personal account identifiers in records where they are no longer required.

Deletion may also remove your ability to sign in and view active subscriptions, token balances, tickets, reservation history and notifications. Please contact the canteen before requesting deletion if you need to resolve an outstanding subscription or reservation.

4. Information we may retain

Some records may be retained for a limited period where necessary to comply with legal or accounting obligations, maintain transaction and audit records, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. This may include limited subscription/payment confirmation records, token or reservation transaction records, and security logs. Where reasonably possible, retained records will be restricted or de-identified and will not be used to restore your deleted account.

5. Processing time

We aim to acknowledge and process verified deletion requests within [X] days, subject to applicable legal requirements and any necessary identity verification. If some information must be retained, we will retain only what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose and for the required period.
