Privacy Policy

Shetty's Canteen mobile application

Effective date: [31 October 2026]   |   Last updated: [31 October 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how Shetty's Canteen ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the Shetty's Canteen mobile application ("App"). The App connects customers with Shetty's Canteen for viewing menu information, requesting subscriptions and reserving meals.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: first name, surname, email address, mobile number, gender (if provided), and password credentials. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are not stored as readable text.
  • Email verification information: verification codes and related verification records used to verify your email address and help protect account access.
  • Profile information: profile photograph or other information you choose to add to your profile.
  • Subscription information: subscription requests, plan, approval status, start and end dates, token allocation, token usage and remaining token balance.
  • Reservation information: meal type (such as lunch or dinner), reservation date and time, ticket identifier, and ticket status, including active, used or expired.
  • Payment records: records of subscription payment confirmation and related transaction details entered or verified by the canteen. Subscription payments may be made directly to the canteen outside the App; the App does not itself process or collect card or bank credentials for such offline payments.
  • Device and notification information: device or app identifiers and push-notification tokens needed to deliver app notifications.
  • Technical and security information: IP address, device/app information, logs, timestamps, and diagnostic or error information where available, for security, troubleshooting and service reliability.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us for help.

2. How we use information

We use information to:

  • create and manage customer and canteen-owner accounts;
  • verify email addresses, authenticate users and secure accounts;
  • display canteen details, menu items and prices;
  • process subscription requests and allow the canteen owner to verify offline payments and approve or reject subscriptions;
  • allocate and maintain tokens, enforce daily usage rules, and maintain accurate token records;
  • create and manage meal reservations and tickets, and record redemption, cancellation or expiry;
  • send service notifications about subscriptions, reservations, tokens and account activity;
  • respond to support requests, diagnose errors, prevent fraud or misuse, and improve the App;
  • comply with applicable laws and protect our rights, users and services.

3. When information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as reasonably necessary:

  • With Shetty's Canteen's authorised owner or staff: customer account, contact, subscription, token and reservation details needed to manage subscriptions and serve reserved meals.
  • With service providers: providers that support the App, such as Supabase (database hosting), Brevo (email delivery), Firebase (push notifications), Cloudinary (image storage), and Render (backend hosting). They process information on our behalf to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and safeguards.
  • For legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is required by law, legal process, or is reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, protect users, or protect our rights and systems.
  • Business changes: if the business or service is reorganised or transferred, subject to appropriate protections and applicable law.

4. Permissions and optional information

The App may request access to your device's photo library or camera if you choose to upload or take a profile image. You can decline this permission; some image-related features may then be unavailable. The App may use notification permissions to send service updates. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings.

5. Data storage and security

Information is stored and processed using the App's hosting and service providers. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, which may include encrypted network connections, access controls, password hashing, authentication, rate limiting and security logging. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot promise that information will never be accessed, lost or disclosed despite our safeguards.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain account and reservation history, administer subscriptions and resolve disputes. Certain transaction, security, audit or legally required records may be retained for longer where necessary to meet legal obligations, prevent fraud, enforce agreements or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or anonymise it.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information. You may update available profile details in the App, manage notification permissions through your device, or request account deletion using the process described on our Account Deletion page. Deleting an account may mean you can no longer access subscriptions, tickets or account history through the App.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not intended to be used by children below the age at which they can independently consent to the processing of personal information under applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

9. Third-party services

The App relies on third-party infrastructure and service providers. Their processing of information may also be governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the privacy information made available by those providers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices or applicable legal requirements change. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the "Last updated" date. Where required, we will provide additional notice in the App.
